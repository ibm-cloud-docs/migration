# Understanding roles and responsibilities in {{site.data.keyword.mh_short}}
{: #roles-responsibilities}

Identify key stakeholders and their responsibilities across planning, execution, and post-migration phases for a successful IBM Cloud migration.
{: shortdesc}

## Roles overview
{: #roles-overview}

The following table describes each role and their key responsibilities.

| Team | Role | Key responsibilities |
| ---- | ---- | ----------------- |
| {{site.data.keyword.mh_short}} Product Team | Builds and maintain the {{site.data.keyword.mh_short}} platform | - Design and deliver the {{site.data.keyword.mh_short}} experience, including in-task support entry points \n - Ensure the Support Center can receive and route migration-originated requests \n - Define & curate the Service team engagement process \n - Publish {{site.data.keyword.mh_short}} data for service team consumption |
| Service Product Team | Own the migration, defines requirements, communicates deadlines | - Define migration requirements, scope, and customer deadlines \n - Author and publish migration topics in the {{site.data.keyword.mh_short}} through the service engagement process \n - Provide technical support and engagement with customers using their migration topics \n - Monitor migration completion rates and adjust communications as needed |
| IBM Cloud Customer | Complete the migration | - Review migration tasks and understand requirements and deadlines \n - Complete required migration steps within the {{site.data.keyword.mh_short}} \n - Raise blockers or issues as needed for the migration topics \n - Validate that migrated resources are functioning correctly post-migration |
