## October 2026
{: #migration-oct2026}
{: release-note}

New! {{site.data.keyword.cloud_notm}} {{site.data.keyword.mh_short}} is now available
:   {{site.data.keyword.mh_short}} is now available in the {{site.data.keyword.cloud_notm}} console. Your centralized platform for managing migrations across {{site.data.keyword.cloud_notm}} products and services. It identifies the resources in your account that require action, helps you plan your migration process, and guides you through each step — with instructions, documentation, and tools for transitions such as deprecated services to modern replacements.

Initially 3 services are included in the {{site.data.keyword.mh_short}} launch topics

- **Virtual Servers migration**: Move Classic virtual server instances to VPC with profile-matched sizing, image capture tooling, and validated cutover steps. For more information, see [Compute servers](/migration/compute-servers/get-started).

- **Containers migration**: Migrate Red Hat OpenShift Kubernetes Service or IBM Kubernetes Service clusters from Classic to VPC-native networking, with guidance on Landing Zone setup, OADP-based workload migration, a migration wizard, and full testing and validation instructions. For more information, see [Containers](/migration/clusters/get-started).

- **Databases migration**: Move to {{site.data.keyword.cloud_notm}} Databases on Gen 2 VPC architecture, with per-engine migration approaches, private endpoint configuration, and automated operations from day one. For more information, see [Data services](/migration/data-services/get-started).
