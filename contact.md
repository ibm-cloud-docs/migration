# Get help with {{site.data.keyword.cloud_notm}} {{site.data.keyword.mh_short}}
{: #help}

Find resources to get help with {{site.data.keyword.cloud_notm}} {{site.data.keyword.mh_short}}, including contact options, support tickets, and links to additional IBM Cloud support resources.
{: shortdesc}

## Getting support
{: #getting-support}

### Step 1: Engage the help popup
{: #engage-help-popup}

You can launch the **Migration help** popup from multiple locations within {{site.data.keyword.mh_short}}. The popup captures the context of your current operation so that support requests include relevant details.

### Step 2: Self-help deflection
{: #self-help-deflection}

After you launch the help popup, browse self-service resources before you open a case. The **Need help with your migration?** panel provides the following tabs:

- **Guidance** — Find guidance, tutorials, and community resources to help you move forward faster.
- **Next Steps** — Explore recommended resources, including the following categories:
    - **Documentation** — Migration guides, prerequisites, best practices, and troubleshooting resources.
    - **Tutorials** — Step-by-step walkthroughs to help you plan, execute, and validate your migration.
    - **Community** — Discussions, migration experiences, and solutions shared by other users.

### Step 3: Support selection
{: #support-selection}

If self-service resources do not resolve your issue, click **I still need help** to proceed to the support case flow. The **Need additional assistance?** panel appears and explains the following information:

- Many migration challenges can be resolved through self-service resources.
- If your question remains unanswered, or you need assistance with a specific issue, you can create a support case to connect with the support team.
- When you create a support case, provide details about your migration scenario, environment, and any troubleshooting steps that you already completed.
- This information helps the support team understand your issue more quickly.

Click **Continue to Support** to proceed.

### Step 4: Support case
{: #normal-case-screen}

The support case form is limited to the {{site.data.keyword.mh_short}} topic only. The case form pre-fills with the following suggested content:

- **Topic**: Migration Hub
- **Subject**: Problem or question with a Migration Hub topic (for example, Containers)
- **Description**: Contextual prompts that ask about the resources you were trying to migrate, the instructions or migration process you were using, and the problem or question you have

### Support routing
{: #support-routing}

After you submit a case, the support system determines the following actions:

- Decides whether the issue belongs to the Migration Hub team or to one of the service teams.
- Routes the case to the appropriate team.
