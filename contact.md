# {{site.data.keyword.cloud_notm}} {{site.data.keyword.mh_short}} support and migration hub help
{: #help}

Find IBM Cloud migration hub support resources, including contact options, support tickets, and IBM Cloud support links to help you complete your migration.
{: shortdesc}

## Getting support
{: #getting-support}

You can launch the **Migration help** popup within {{site.data.keyword.mh_short}} and browse self-help resources, like migration guides, prerequisites, best practices, and troubleshooting resources.
   
### Support selection
{: #support-selection}

If self-help resources do not resolve your issue, click **I still need help** to proceed to the support case flow. Click **Continue to Support** to proceed.

### Support case
{: #normal-case-screen}

The support case form is limited to the {{site.data.keyword.mh_short}} topic only. The case form pre-fills with the following suggested content:

- **Topic**: {{site.data.keyword.mh_short}}
- **Subject**: Problem or question with a {{site.data.keyword.mh_short}} topic (for example, Containers)
- **Description**: Contextual prompts that ask about the resources you were trying to migrate, the instructions or migration process you were using, and the problem or question you have.

You can also provide any evidence of issues through **attachments** and add additional email Ids to receive any notications regarding the support case.

Adding a user's email address to the watchlist sends email notifications to the user when the case is updated, but does not grant access to view the case. To follow the case link in the email notification and view the case, the account owner must use IBM Cloud® Identity and Access Management (IAM) access policies to grant the user case view access.
{: note}

Click **Next**, review the support case and **Submit**.

### Support routing
{: #support-routing}

After you submit a case, the support system determines the following actions:

- Decides whether the issue belongs to the {{site.data.keyword.mh_short}} team or to one of the service teams.
- Routes the case to the appropriate team.
